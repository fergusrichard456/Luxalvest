Who we are

Lux Alvest Capital is a Jakarta-based boutique advisory and proprietary investment firm.

We advise business owners, entrepreneurs, and companies on mergers & acquisitions, capital raisings, restructurings, and pre- and post-IPO mandates. Alongside our advisory practice, we invest our own capital in businesses and opportunities where we believe we can contribute to long-term value creation.

This dual perspective shapes how we work. We approach every mandate not only as an adviser, but with the discipline of an owner — asking whether the decisions we recommend can create durable value for the people who built, operate, and own the business.

Rooted in Indonesia, we believe capital should do more than complete transactions. It should help good businesses grow, create opportunity, and contribute to a more prosperous Indonesia.
