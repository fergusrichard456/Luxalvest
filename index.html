Who we are

Lux Alvest Capital is a Jakarta-based investment and advisory firm assisting founders, boards, and shareholders in the design, structuring, and execution of mergers & acquisitions, capital raisings, restructurings, and pre- and post-IPO mandates — investing its own capital alongside the companies it believes in.

Meet our team
What we do

Along every stage of the transaction lifecycle.

From the decision to transact, to the structure, the numbers, and the negotiation in between.

Deal Strategy

We help owners decide whether, when, and what to transact — M&A strategy, commercial due diligence, and project feasibility studies grounded in first principles.

Corporate Finance

We structure and execute capital events: capital restructurings, IPO and capital raising, and private equity transactions — from mandate to close.

Transaction Services

We run the transaction machinery — structuring, financial and tax due diligence, and sale & purchase agreement review — so surprises surface before signing, not after.

Business Valuations

We build the number the negotiation stands on: business and share valuations, and the valuation models behind them.
